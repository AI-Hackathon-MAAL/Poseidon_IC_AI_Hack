About this workspace

Soundings helps analysts investigate historical vessel activity around marine protected areas. Select an area, choose an observation and run the agent to prepare a cited brief.

Priorities describe the next review action. Recorded positions and published reports supply the evidence. Vessel activity during a tracking gap remains unknown.

Use Tab to navigate controls. The map supports its own camera and layer controls. All event times use UTC.
