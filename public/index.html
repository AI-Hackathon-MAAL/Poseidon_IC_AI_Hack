About Poseidon

Poseidon is a maritime investigation agent for conservation analysts. Select a case and choose Run investigation. Poseidon checks the vessel record, looks up the fishing rules for the area and date, and prepares a brief with sources for your review.

Priorities describe the next review action. Recorded positions and published reports supply the evidence. Vessel activity during a tracking gap remains unknown.

All event times use UTC. Use the map’s controls to explore the globe or focus on an area.
