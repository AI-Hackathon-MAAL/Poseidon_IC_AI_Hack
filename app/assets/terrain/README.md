# Regional relief

These tiles use Natural Earth’s public-domain **Gray Earth with Shaded Relief, Hypsography, and Ocean Bottom**, large, version 3.2.0. Land relief comes from SRTM Plus and ocean relief from CleanTOPO2. The tiles provide cartographic context for recorded vessel positions. [Dataset and credits](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-gray-earth/gray-earth-with-shaded-relief-and-ocean-bottom/) · [Natural Earth terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/)

The 21,600 × 10,800 equirectangular source is divided into 24 columns and 12 rows from the northwest corner at 180° W, 90° N. Each 900 × 900 WebP tile covers 15° × 15°. Land and water are tinted to match the globe. Coastlines and seafloor relief come from the raster.
